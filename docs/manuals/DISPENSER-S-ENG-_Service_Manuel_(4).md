 6. PRODUCT INSTALLATION AND HANDLING INFORMATION
The product consists of 2 parts, the carrier leg and the machine part. These two parts are sent to the
customer in wooden cases as fully assembled. The customer can get the machine ready for use by
simply combining these two parts.

Pay attention to the following points during the transport process.
   1. The carrier leg and the machine part should be carried separately and combined in the area
       to be used.
   2. The carrier foot should be carried by 2 people by holding it from the bottom part.
   3. The machine part should be carried by 2 people by holding it from the right and left sides of
       the machine.

Follow the steps below while installing.
    1. Place the carriage on a flat surface with the stand upright. Pay attention to this issue as the
        uneven surface will cause overturning.
    2. Remove the protective washers from the end of the support legs using 6 Allen Allen.
    3. Hold the machine part by the ends of 2 people.
    4. Insert the arms of the carrier foot into the carrier slots of the machine.
    5. Make sure that the carrier arms are fully inserted into the carrier holes.
    6. Replace the protective washers you removed in Step 2.
    7. Insert the package you will use into the package slot. For detailed label placement
        information, you can check the sections 9.2 and 9.3.2 of the user manual.
    8. After these steps, the product is ready for use. You can create a label signal by using the
        photocell that comes mounted on the machine.

Dispenser performs the labeling process according to the settings related to the position of the label.




                                          Figure 6 Labelling Settings




   1. Label Speed: Used to determine the speed of the labeling process. Its maximum value is

                                    This field contains the parameters that allow us to choose whether

                                    dispenser from section 1.5.16

                                    2. External signal duty: It is used when external signal input is
                                    activated. It ignores the signals that will come to the machine during
                                    the value entered in this parameter. Its unit is ms. External signal
                                    duty time is not smaller than this value to print. Otherwise, no
                                    printing process. Standard duty time is 2 ms. The duty time cannot
                                    be more than 600 ms and less than 0 ms. This parameter is used
                                    to prevent undesired signal. Following sample is valid for the value
                                    is equal to 2 ms. The working principle of external signal is shown
                                    in Figure 11. This value can be a maximum of 600 and a minimum
                                    of 0.

                                    3. Internal signal: It is used when the machine is required to
                                    receive a contact signal from the internal signal source depending
                                    on the package length.
